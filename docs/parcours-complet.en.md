# A mobility from end to end

This page follows **one whole mobility**, from the first setting to the transcript, saying at
each step **who acts** and **what triggers the next one**.

The rest of the documentation describes screens one by one. This one describes the sequence —
which is often what is missing when you are looking for why a file is not moving.

## Before any campaign: the groundwork

Nothing below works if these four things do not exist.

| What you need | Who prepares it | Without it |
|---|---|---|
| **Your partners** and their **agreements** | Coordinator | No destination to offer |
| **Places** set in the matrix | Coordinator | Destinations exist but open nothing |
| **Your level labels** (*Custom label*: one per cohort, or one per level), **carried by each student** | Administrator, in **Settings ▸ Academic levels** | The matching round refuses to start |
| **Your mobility periods** | Administrator | Places have no column to sit in |

!!! tip "The most common false start"
    Creating a campaign before setting any places. The campaign opens, students see it, and
    find no destination. Check the matrix before you open.

## 1. The campaign opens

**Who acts:** the coordinator.

They create the campaign, choose **who takes part** and **where one can go**, set the number
of wishes, the required documents and the few options that cannot be revisited later. See
[Setting up a campaign, setting by setting](etablissement/campagnes.md).

The campaign moves from **Draft** to **Ready**, then **Opened**. A **Ready** campaign opens
on its own at its opening time, and students are then invited; a campaign left in **Draft**
never opens by itself.

**What triggers the next step:** only the **Opened** state accepts wishes. A "Ready" campaign
is already visible to students, but it cannot be filled in.

## 2. The student places their wishes

**Who acts:** the student.

They browse destinations, set some aside as favourites, and pour them into their wishes,
ranked by preference. They only see destinations where a place is planned for them. While
the campaign is open, they can change their list.

If you set a **minimum number of wishes**, their list does not save until they reach it. If
you enabled the "I do not wish to go" answer, they can also give it — without having to
justify themselves.

**What triggers the next step:** the campaign closing, at its closing time or by your hand.
Their wishes are then frozen.

## 3. Placement

**Who acts:** the coordinator.

Two paths, which coexist.

**The automatic round** (**Match all students**) serves students in ranking order: the
first-ranked gets their best available wish, and what is left goes to the next. It requires
a profile, a level label and an imported ranking from every participant — see
[What matching requires](etablissement/campagnes.md). It can be re-run as often as needed.
It produces **system proposals**, which you confirm one by one.

**Placing by hand** lets you seat whoever you want, wherever you want, ranked or not — with
three limits: a term is required, a student already nominated cannot be reassigned, and if
the campaign requires a complete file, it requires it by hand too.

**What triggers the next step:** **Send proposals** sends the confirmed proposals to the
students.

## 4. The student accepts — or not

**Who acts:** the student, unless you chose direct assignment.

They receive their proposal and accept or decline it; an email confirms their answer.
Declining also closes their other wishes in the campaign. If you set the campaign to
**direct assignment**, this step disappears: sending creates the placement.

**What triggers the next step:** acceptance.

## 5. Nominating to the partner

**Who acts:** the coordinator, then the host institution.

You nominate the student to their destination; this needs a contact at the partner. The
partner answers through the platform or the EWP network, and their answer records itself.
If they confirm by email or phone, **Confirm manually** records it, stating the channel.

While they have not answered, you can **remind** them (once every twenty-four hours) or
**withdraw** the nomination, which makes the student available for another destination.

A refusal always comes with a reason, passed on to the institution that nominated.

**What triggers the next step:** the partner's confirmation. If a
[mobility path](etablissement/parcours.md) is plugged into the campaign, the student joins
it at that point: it supplies the tasks of the next step.

## 6. The file fills up

**Who acts:** the student uploads, the coordinator validates.

Contractual documents, language scores, learning agreement. Every expected item is listed,
and the student sees what is missing. You remind the stragglers from the selection bar of
your list, without keeping a separate list.

An incomplete file counts from placement onwards (step 3): depending on the campaign
setting, it either **blocks** it or is merely **flagged**.

**What triggers the next step:** the file is complete, and the dates are near.

## 7. Departure, then the stay

**Who acts:** everyone, a little at a time.

The student leaves. Their learning agreement can be changed along the way — any component
change asks for a reason. The period's deadlines appear in the
[agenda](etablissement/agenda.md), and in whichever calendar each person subscribed to.

## 8. Coming back

**Who acts:** the student, then their institution.

The student enters the grade obtained for each course of their learning agreement, picks
the grading system and can attach the host institution's official **transcript**. Their
institution validates it or asks for a correction.

The student also submits their **feedback** on the host institution — and may allow their
photos to be published. Feedback is moderated in your
[validations](etablissement/validations.md) before it is published. Those who have not
answered are reminded like documents.

**What closes the loop:** feedback feeds the partner's record, which the next students will
read before placing their wishes.
