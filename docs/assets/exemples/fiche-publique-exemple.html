Overview

Brief description
Founded in 1911, the University of Lisbon Atlantic is a public research university welcoming 28,000 students, including 4,500 international students from 90 countries. Its three campuses are located in the heart of Lisbon, close to the Tagus river.

Location
Lisbon, capital of Portugal, 550,000 inhabitants. Main campus in the Alvalade district, 15 minutes by metro from the city center. Mild climate, over 290 days of sunshine a year. Relaxed, safe and very student-friendly atmosphere.

Academic

Academic System

Two semesters: September to January and February to June. ECTS credits: 30 per semester. Grading from 0 to 20 (10 is the pass mark). Continuous assessment plus final exams; attendance is mandatory at 75% of classes.

Courses and Programs

Faculties: Business and Economics, Engineering, Social Sciences, Law, Arts and Humanities, Sciences. Exchange students can pick courses across faculties (up to 20% outside their home field). Popular programs: International Management, Data Science, Architecture, Marine Biology.

Enrolment Procedures and Required Documents

Steps: 1) Nomination by the home institution, 2) Online application on the partner portal, 3) Learning Agreement signed by all parties, 4) Acceptance letter issued. Documents: passport copy, transcript of records, language certificate, learning agreement, ID photo. Deadlines: 15 June (fall semester), 15 November (spring semester).

Student life

Campus Life

Modern libraries open until midnight, sports center, 80+ student associations, free Wi-Fi, open-air cafeterias. Very safe campus with 24/7 security. A dedicated international student lounge is open daily.

Welcome and Support Services

Orientation week in early September and early February, airport pick-up service, buddy program pairing each exchange student with a local student, free tutoring and academic writing workshops, a dedicated welcome desk on the main campus.

Climate & Lifestyle

Hot, dry summers (up to 32°C) and mild, rainy winters (8 to 15°C). Dinner is eaten late (after 20:00), lunch is a long break, and life is largely outdoors. Cost of living is lower than in most Western European capitals.

Cultural and Social Activities

Weekly language tandems, Erasmus Student Network (ESN) trips to Sintra, Porto and the Algarve, fado nights, street festivals (Santos Populares in June), film clubs and a very active nightlife in Bairro Alto and Cais do Sodré.

Practical info

Finance and scholarships

Tuition Fees and Scholarships

No tuition fees for exchange students (waived under the exchange agreement). Erasmus+ grant: 520 EUR per month for Portugal (group 2). Additional Erasmus+ top-up for students with fewer opportunities: 250 EUR per month. Local merit scholarship: 1,000 EUR per semester, 15 awarded per year.
Logistics and organization

Cost of Living

Average monthly budget: 900 to 1,200 EUR. Housing 400 to 600 EUR, food 200 to 250 EUR, transport pass 30 EUR (under 23), leisure 100 to 150 EUR. Student canteen meal: 3 EUR.

Housing & Accommodation

Options: university residences (3 residences, 350 to 450 EUR per month, 15 to 30 minutes from campus), private shared flats (400 to 600 EUR), studios (650 to 900 EUR). Apply for residences by 1 July. Tip: avoid signing a lease before arrival and use the university housing office to prevent scams.

Insurance & Health

Universal public health system (SNS). EU students: European Health Insurance Card (EHIC). Non-EU students: private health insurance mandatory (around 40 EUR per month). A medical center on the main campus offers free first consultations.

Visa & Administrative Procedures

EU students: no visa, registration at the town hall if staying over 3 months. Non-EU students: student visa to apply for at the Portuguese consulate 2 to 3 months before departure (processing 4 to 8 weeks, fee 90 EUR). Required: acceptance letter, proof of funds, insurance, accommodation proof.

Useful Contacts

International Relations Office: Avenida da Liberdade 120, 1250-142 Lisboa. Email: incoming@ulisboa-atlantic.example. Phone: +351 21 000 00 00. Website: www.ulisboa-atlantic.example/international. Office hours: Monday to Friday, 9:30 to 16:00.

Practical Information Before Departure

Budget at least 1,500 EUR for the first month (deposit included). Bring warm clothes for winter evenings and a rain jacket. Arrive 3 to 5 days before orientation week. Keep digital and paper copies of all documents. Get a Portuguese SIM card and set up a Revolut/Wise account.
Frequently asked questions
What language level do I need to follow the courses?
Courses taught in English require B2: IELTS 6.5, TOEFL iBT 85, or Cambridge C1 Advanced at 180. Courses taught in French require B2 as well. The test must be less than two years old. If your university certifies your level in writing, we accept its statement instead of a test.
How many credits must I take each semester?
Between 24 and 36 ECTS per semester, 30 being the normal load. Below 24 your learning agreement cannot be approved. The course change window closes two weeks after the start of term; after that no change is possible.
Do I need a visa?
EU nationals do not. Everyone else needs a student visa, and processing takes six to eight weeks. Our acceptance letter, which you receive as soon as your nomination is approved, is one of the required documents. Apply the day you have it in hand.
Is health insurance compulsory?
Yes, without exception. The European Health Insurance Card is enough for EU nationals. Everyone else must show international insurance covering medical care, hospitalisation and repatriation for the whole stay. We check the certificate on arrival; without it, course registration cannot be completed.
What does living here cost?
Budget EUR 850 to 1,100 per month, housing included. A meal in the university restaurant is EUR 3.30, and a transport pass EUR 25 per month if you are under 26. No tuition fee is due: you stay enrolled, and paying, at your home university.
Do you offer housing to exchange students?
We hold 40 rooms in university residences for exchange students, allocated in the order requests arrive. Expect EUR 380 to 520 per month, utilities included. Apply as soon as you receive your acceptance letter; after 30 June for the autumn semester we can no longer guarantee a room and will point you to our list of partner private residences.
Is there a welcome programme on arrival?
A welcome week runs before each term: campus tour, help with paperwork, opening a bank account, and a student buddy for every arrival. It is not compulsory, but everything is settled in five days — expect three times longer if you arrive after it.
When must I nominate my students?
15 May for the autumn semester, 31 October for spring. Students then have until 15 June, respectively 30 November, to submit their application. These dates are firm: a late nomination can no longer be processed before the start of term.
How will my transcript of records reach me?
We issue it within six weeks of the end of the examination period and send it straight to your home university, in ECTS. You receive a copy. If a course title needs clarifying for your credit recognition, write to us: we answer within five working days.
Where can I go if my destination is not on the list?
Our agreements cover 41 institutions across four continents. If your field is not represented, book an appointment with the office: we open two or three new agreements each year on students' proposals, provided the case is made before December.
Is my grant paid before I leave?
An advance of 70% is paid once your grant agreement and learning agreement are signed, usually in the month before departure. The balance follows on your return, after we receive your certificate of departure and your transcript of records. Without those two documents the balance stays blocked.
Can I change courses once I am there?
Yes, during the change window set by the host institution, usually the first two weeks. Every change goes through an amendment to your learning agreement, signed by all three parties. A course taken outside the agreement will not be recognised on your return.
