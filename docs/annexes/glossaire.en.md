# Glossary

The acronyms and terms you meet across AroundLink and international mobility.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **IR office** | The international relations office of a school or university — the team this documentation is written for. |
| **IRO** | *International Relations Office* — the team that manages mobility at an institution. |
| **DRI / Head of IR** | The strategic steering of international mobility. |
| **Erasmus+** | The European Union's mobility programme, which funds student exchanges among other things. |
| **EWP** | *Erasmus Without Paper* — the European network for digital exchange of mobility data between institutions. See [EWP network](../ewp/ewp.md). |
| **IIA** | *Inter-Institutional Agreement* — the Erasmus+ agreement signed between two universities. |
| **Bilateral agreement** | A direct cooperation agreement (outside Erasmus/EWP) negotiated between two institutions. |
| **Double degree** | A joint programme where the student earns a degree at both institutions. |
| **Paying mobility** | A *study abroad* mobility where the student pays fees to the host institution. |
| **OLA** | *Online Learning Agreement* — the digital learning agreement: the list of courses taken abroad and their recognition. |
| **Learning Agreement** | The learning agreement (see OLA). |
| **ToR** | *Transcript of Records* — the official transcript of the mobility. |
| **ECTS** | *European Credit Transfer System* — the European credit system (30 ECTS ≈ one semester). |
| **Factsheet** | An institution's practical fact sheet (deadlines, housing/visa contacts…), exchanged over EWP. |
| **Nomination** | The official proposal of a student to a host institution, which accepts or rejects it. |
| **Affectation / placement** | A student's confirmed placement on a destination, within a campaign. |
| **Wish** | A destination choice ranked by the student during a campaign. |
| **Campaign** | A framed application round (dates, levels, number of wishes) run by the IRO. |
| **Place distribution** | The breakdown of an agreement's places by year, level, level label, period, field and campus (*Place Distribution* screen). |
| **CNR** | *Change Notification Receiver* — the EWP notification telling a partner that data changed. |
| **HEI** | *Higher Education Institution*; the *HEI ID* is its EWP identifier. |
| **SCHAC** | A standard institution identifier (based on its domain). |
| **Erasmus code** | An institution's official Erasmus code. |
| **ROR** | *Research Organization Registry* — a global open registry of research/education organisations, the basis of [Atlas](../atlas/atlas.md). |
| **ECHE** | *Erasmus Charter for Higher Education* — the charter that accredits an institution for the programme. |
| **EQF** | *European Qualifications Framework* — the European framework of qualification levels (e.g. EQF 6 = bachelor, 7 = master, 8 = doctorate). |
| **ISCED** | The international classification of fields of education (discipline). |
| **OUnit** | *Organizational Unit* — a faculty/department of an institution, in the EWP sense. |
| **Mobility grant** | The Erasmus+ financial support paid to the student. See [Mobility grants](../etablissement/bourse.md). |
| **Beneficiary Module** | The official Erasmus+ tool where an institution reports its mobilities. |
| **SSO** | *Single Sign-On* — signing in with an existing account, Microsoft or Google. |
| **Mobility path** | The sequence of steps a student follows before, during and after their mobility (*Workflow*). It is built in **Settings ▸ Workflows** and plugged into a campaign (*Mobility workflow*) or a welcome session. See [Mobility paths](../etablissement/parcours.md). |
| **Phase** | The stage of the mobility a student is in: before, during, after, closed. |
| **Welcome session** | The group of incoming students following the same path. See [Onboarding](../etablissement/onboarding.md). |
| **Public page** | What your partners and their students read about your institution. See [Your public page](../etablissement/fiche-publique.md). |
| **Requirement** | A condition set by a destination — language level, document — that a student must meet to ask for it (*Requirements per destination*). |
| **Direct assignment** | A campaign setting where the coordinator's decision creates the placement, without the student accepting. |
| **Class rank** | Each student's imported rank in their cohort, which the matching round uses to serve students in order. |
| **Multi-tenant (SaaS)** | The architecture where each client institution is isolated from others on a shared platform. |

## Words that get confused

Eight pairs of terms that look alike and do not mean the same thing. They are by far the
first source of misunderstanding between a team and its tool.

### Wish, proposal, placement, nomination

Four words for four distinct moments of the same file, in this order:

| Term | Who issues it | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Wish** | The student | A destination they ask for, ranked by preference |
| **Proposal** | The tool suggests it, the coordinator confirms and sends it | A destination offered to them, which they can still decline |
| **Placement** | The coordinator, or the student's acceptance | The destination retained — the file has a seat |
| **Nomination** | The coordinator, towards the partner | The official announcement to the host institution |

A student may have five wishes, one proposal, one placement and one nomination. It is not the
same thing in four places: it is four steps.

### Academic level and level label

The **academic level** is a shared European scale — bachelor, master, doctorate. It lets you
compare two students from two countries.

The **level label** (*Custom label*) is the name *you* give your cohorts: "Aero 4", "MSc
26-27". It only means something at home, it is set in **Settings ▸ Academic levels**, each
student carries their own, and it is what designates the cell in the place matrix.

That is why the matching round requires a level label and not just a level: "Aero 4" and
"Aero 5" are both master's, and do not open the same places.

Not to be confused with the **mobility path**, which is a sequence of steps.

### Tag and student filter

A **tag** stays with you. A **student filter** can be shown to your students, who use it to
search for a destination — and it also **restricts** what they can ask for: when a student
and a partner both carry filters, they must share at least one. See
[Tag or student filter?](../etablissement/parametres.md).

### Agreement, exchange, place

The **agreement** is the contract binding you to a partner — it has a type, dates, signatories.

The **exchange** is a line of that agreement: a direction, a level, a field.

The **place** is an available seat, for a given period and level label. It is what the student
occupies. An agreement can exist without opening a single place.

### Outgoing and incoming

**Outgoing**: your students leaving. **Incoming**: the students you host.

**Place counters cover outgoing.** An agreement often opens places both ways; only those
you send count towards your totals, and the agreement shows an incoming gauge separately.

### Draft, Ready and Opened

A **Draft** campaign is only visible to your team. A **Ready** campaign is already visible to
your students, and opens on its own at its opening time. Only an **Opened** campaign accepts
their wishes. This confusion explains most of the "my students see nothing": the campaign was
left in Draft.

### Access opened and account connected

**Access opened**: you sent their invitation. **Connected**: they used it. Two separate
columns, because they call for two different reminders.

### Client partner and non-client partner

A partner that uses AroundLink itself **publishes** its information: its factsheet, course
catalogue, library, FAQ. You read them as they write them, and they update by themselves.

A partner that does not use it publishes nothing. What you write about them is then **yours**,
and your institution alone sees it.
