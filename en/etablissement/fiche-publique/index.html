    Your public page¶

    Your public page is a web page in your name, reachable without an account, that you compose from AroundLink and hand to whoever you like: a partner discovering you, a student hesitating, a colleague preparing a committee.

    It reuses what you already maintain in the tool — your photos, your mobility periods, your language requirements, your course catalogue, your FAQ. Nothing to retype.

    Where it happens

    My institution ▸ Public page. The page's address is app.aroundlink.com/aroundlink/<your-school>.

    Composing, not filling in a form¶

    What it is for. Deciding what the world sees of you, while seeing it.

    Who it is for. IO officer / coordinator

    How it works. The composer shows the page itself, exactly as the public will see it. Each block can be grabbed and moved, or removed with a cross; a side panel offers what is not yet placed, sorted by category and collapsible.

    The preview is the page: there is no "edit mode" that would render differently from reality.

    The order of sections cannot be changed

    That is deliberate: every institution's page reads in the same order, which makes it comparable from one partner to the next. You decide what gets published, not the order.

    What you can put on it¶

    How it works. Your logo is served automatically, at the top and in the footer: it is not a block to tick. The rest is your choice.

    Group Available blocks
    Identity & media Photos · Key facts (website, Erasmus code, accreditations) · Campus cards
    Academic data Mobility periods · Language requirements · Course catalogue · Catalogue filter by promotion
    Your sections every section of your student content, in the order of your categories — general information, academic aspects, student life, scholarships, logistics
    FAQ your frequently asked questions, which close the page
    Side panel Nomination and application deadlines · Team contacts (role and email only) · Postal address · Student reviews (no names) · Useful links

    What never goes out

    Team contacts only expose the role and the email address — never a personal number. Student reviews are published without their author's name. And no content belonging to another institution appears on your page.

    Your colours¶

    How it works. You set two colours — an accent colour and a dark one — and the page follows: the banner, the buttons, the footer. With your logo opening and closing the page, it looks like your institution, not like AroundLink.

    A discreet Powered by AroundLink signature sits at the bottom.

    One page per campus¶

    What it is for. An institution with several sites has more than one reality: courses, periods and contacts may differ from one city to the next.

    How it works. Each declared campus can have its own page, composed separately, and a selector moves from one to another in the composer. Content is broken down by campus: a course declares which campuses it is taught on, and a campus page only shows its own.

    On the public side, a menu links the pages together. A campus address is shared as you are looking at it.

    Publishing, sharing, switching off¶

    How it works. One switch publishes the page. The address copies with a button.

    The address never regenerates

    That is deliberate: you must be able to leave this link everywhere — in your emails, on your website, in a printed PDF — without it breaking one day. If you no longer want to be visible, switch the page off: that is the fallback, and the link works again when you switch it back on.

    Use case.

    The office composes its page in twenty minutes: photos, periods, language requirements, course catalogue, FAQ, and in the side panel the two nomination deadlines. It sets the school's colours, publishes, and pastes the address into its email signature. A partner asking "your dates and prerequisites?" now gets a link instead of a PDF.
